We take a whole-person approach to oral and facial muscle function. By retraining the tongue, lips, and breathing patterns, we help correct the root causes behind sleep, posture, and developmental concerns — not just the symptoms.

We work hand in hand with your dentists, orthodontists, ENTs, and physicians, so your care is coordinated and effective. The result is a stronger foundation for nasal breathing, proper tongue rest posture, and lifelong oral health.

Why nasal breathing matters

Breathing through the nose filters, warms, and humidifies air, and boosts oxygen uptake. It supports deeper sleep, calmer focus, and proper facial and airway development — while chronic mouth breathing is linked to snoring, fatigue, and dental issues.

Why tongue function matters

When the tongue rests on the roof of the mouth, it guides jaw growth, keeps teeth aligned, and supports an open airway. Correcting tongue posture and swallowing helps with speech, eating, and lasting orthodontic results.

Why a care team matters

We coordinate with your dentist, orthodontist, ENT, and physician so everyone is working toward the same goals. This team approach treats root causes together and makes every treatment more effective and longer-lasting.

The Basics

What is Myofunctional Therapy?

Myofunctional therapy is gentle, exercise-based training for the muscles of the face, mouth, and tongue. Much like physical therapy retrains your body, it retrains how your tongue rests, how you swallow, and how you breathe — so the right muscles do the right jobs.

The goal is simple: nasal breathing, lips together, and the tongue resting gently on the roof of the mouth. When those everyday patterns are in place, they support healthy jaw growth, straighter teeth, better sleep, and clearer focus — for children and adults alike.

Breathe through the noseReplacing mouth breathing with calm, consistent nasal breathing — day and night.
Proper tongue postureTraining the tongue to rest on the palate, where it guides healthy oral development.
Retrain habitsGently unwinding patterns like thumb-sucking, tongue thrust, and improper swallowing.
A coordinated teamWorking alongside your dentist, orthodontist, and ENT for lasting results.

A typical program is a series of short, easy daily exercises practiced at home, with check-ins along the way. No two plans are identical — yours starts with a free consultation.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the things families ask most. Don't see your question? Reach out — we're happy to help.

What exactly is myofunctional therapy?

It's gentle, exercise-based training for the muscles of the face, mouth, and tongue. We retrain how the tongue rests, how you swallow, and how you breathe — so the right muscles do the right jobs and support healthy growth, sleep, and function.

Who can benefit — is it just for kids?

People of all ages. We work with young children, teens, and adults. If you notice mouth breathing, snoring, tongue thrust, post-orthodontic relapse, or related concerns, therapy may help at any stage of life.

How long does a program take?

Most programs run several months, with short daily exercises at home and regular check-ins. Because every person is different, your exact plan and timeline are set after an initial evaluation.

What happens at the first appointment?

We start with a free consultation to understand your concerns and goals. From there, a full evaluation assesses tongue function, breathing, and oral posture, and we build a personalized plan together.

Do you work with my dentist or orthodontist?

Yes. We partner closely with dentists, orthodontists, ENTs, and physicians so your care is coordinated. Myofunctional therapy often makes other treatments more effective and longer-lasting.

Are sessions in person or online?

Both. We offer in-person visits in Vacaville, Walnut Creek, and Danville, and online telehealth sessions across the Sacramento Valley and SF Bay Area — whichever fits your family best.
