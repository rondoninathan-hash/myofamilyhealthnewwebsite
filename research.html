Myofunctional Disorder Resources

Information for medical professionals and families

This page is a resource guide to help medical professionals and families better understand the root causes of obstructive sleep apnea and sleep-breathing disorders. Parents and adults will also find help understanding tongue ties and their effects on mouth breathing, snoring, and the need for orthodontic work (braces).

The following resources show how myofunctional therapy can address concerns in both adults and children, and share evidence-based research about the services we provide. As an authority in the myofunctional therapy and airway field, Amy Rondoni is here to help you gather information about myofunctional disorders, sleep apnea, mouth breathing, and tongue ties.

The links below lead to third-party research articles and educational resources, provided for informational and educational purposes. Each opens in a new tab.
